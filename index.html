What Was Detected

PA denials from Cigna for etrasimod (VELSIPITY) increased 41% over the trailing 14 days compared to the prior 14-day period. The spike began approximately April 15, 2026. Analysis of 83 Cigna PA-related interactions identified a new step therapy requirement as the primary driver — Cigna now requires documented failure on a TNF inhibitor before approving etrasimod. This requirement affects both new PA submissions and renewals.

Supporting Evidence
Denial Pattern
56 of 83 Cigna PA interactions (67%) this period involved a denial or pending denial citing "step therapy not met." Prior period: 23 of 71 (32%). The denial language specifically references TNF inhibitor documentation.
HCP Office Impact
14 unique HCP offices called DCC seeking updated Cigna PA criteria guidance. 3 offices requested peer-to-peer review scheduling. Offices report receiving denial letters without clear guidance on qualifying step therapy agents and duration.
Patient Downstream Effect
Interim Care Rx inquiries from Cigna patients increased 2.4x. Repeat-contact rate for Cigna PA issues: 28% (vs. 19% baseline). Average days in PA limbo: 16 (vs. 9 prior).
